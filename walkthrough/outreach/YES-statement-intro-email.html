How to use the guided tour

About 40 minutes for all 22 features. You can stop at any time and continue later on the same browser.

1
 Open the guided tour and add your name if you like. It appears next to your votes and comments.
2
 Select Start the walkthrough. Each step highlights a part of the statement and explains what it does and why it matters for YES and for your customers.
3
 Give your view on each feature: Include or Exclude (with a reason if you like), a priority of High, Medium or Low, and an optional comment. Your answers save automatically.
4
 Move around freely with Back, Next and All steps. Restart takes you to the beginning and keeps your answers. You can also click around the statement itself during any step.
5
 Try it on any device with View as Mobile, Tablet or Desktop, or select New tab to open the statement on its own.
6
 See what everyone thinks in Results, with charts, rankings and every comment. Data requirements shows the data each feature would need from YES.

A taste of what is inside

  • A clear balance summary and an interactive balance journey that shows exactly how the balance changed.
  • Every transaction, searchable, with full details, dates, references and fees.
  • “Your statement in 60 seconds”: a narrated, captioned video built from the customer’s own figures, in English and Spanish.
  • Explain with AI and the Ask YES assistant. In this preview they are simulated: no live AI model is used and nothing is sent anywhere.
  • A guided way for customers to ask about a transaction, which can mean fewer calls and disputes.
  • Plain-language help on stablecoins, reserves and transparency.
  • PDF, print and CSV downloads.
  • English and Spanish, dark mode, a layout for any screen, and a design that aims to meet WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility.

Good to know

  • Please share these links with colleagues at YES who should have a say. Everyone can take the tour and vote.
  • Votes and comments are visible to everyone with the link, so please keep comments free of confidential or customer information.
  • Your answers are linked to your browser, not to an account. Use the same browser to continue where you left off.
  • The tour works on phones and tablets, but a laptop or desktop gives the best experience.
