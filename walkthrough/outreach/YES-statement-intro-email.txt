NEW: WHAT'S NEW FROM YES
Your statement is the one message every customer opens. Now it can also introduce what is next from YES, at no extra mailing cost. Every update is clearly labelled "From YES", can be dismissed, and never appears in the PDF, print or CSV, so the statement of record stays factual.

Four updates, personalised from the customer's own statement:
- Pay a YES identity. Send to a YES identity, like yes.danielk, instantly and with no network fee. A customer who paid network fees sending to an external wallet sees exactly what they paid this month, and how to avoid it next time.
- Progressive trust. A Bronze, Silver and Gold meter shows the customer's trust level, what they sent this month against their limit, and the next step to raise it.
- More currencies. Coming soon: hold EUBC, UKBC, MXBC and NZBC alongside USBC.
- Interest on USBC. Coming soon: earn interest on your USBC balance. Subject to regulatory approval. Rates and terms to be confirmed.

Where customers see it:
- On the first screen: a count of new updates on the What's new tab, a blue dot on Ask YES and the phone menu, and a one-line teaser inside the balance card. They clear once the customer has seen the updates.
- On the Overview: a tinted card that leads with the customer's own figure, such as the fees they paid, with a simple visual: a fee comparison, a trust meter or the new currencies. One update at a time, with See what's new, Next update and Not now. It never rotates on its own.
- In context: a short note beside the network fee and in the details of an external wallet send, at the moment it matters most.
- A new What's new tab with all four updates, each with its own link and a New label until it has been seen.
- Ask YES: "What's new?" is the first suggested question, and gives the updates in a few lines.

HOW TO USE THE GUIDED TOUR
About 45 minutes for all 24 features. You can stop at any time and continue later on the same browser.
1. Open the guided tour and add your name if you like. It appears next to your votes and comments.
2. Select Start the walkthrough. Each step highlights a part of the statement and explains what it does and why it matters for YES and for your customers.
3. Give your view on each feature: Include or Exclude (with a reason if you like), a priority of High, Medium or Low, and an optional comment. Your answers save automatically.
4. Move around freely with Back, Next and All steps. Restart takes you to the beginning and keeps your answers. You can also click around the statement itself during any step.
5. Try it on any device with View as Mobile, Tablet or Desktop, or select New tab to open the statement on its own.
6. See what everyone thinks in Results, with charts, rankings and every comment. Data requirements shows the data each feature would need from YES.
